`Formats/AliasInfo.ps1xml` is derived from the built-in
`System.Management.Automation.AliasInfo` view in [PowerShell][powershell],
exported with `Export-FormatData`. The `Description` field was added.

## eza aliases

The `lD`, `la`, `ll` and `ls` aliases in `Alias.ps1` are based on the
[Oh My Zsh `eza` plugin][omz-eza] (MIT License, Copyright (c) Robby Russell
and contributors). `Invoke-Eza` wraps [eza][eza] (EUPL-1.2), which is
installed separately.

## Removing old module versions

The idea for `moduleclean.ps1` came from Jack Fruh's
[Powershell script to remove duplicate, old modules][fruh]. The post states no
license. The current script is an independent rewrite and contains none of
the post's code.
